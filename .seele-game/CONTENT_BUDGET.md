# CONTENT_BUDGET — Signal in the Fog

## Required release content
| Category | Budget | Acceptance note |
|---|---:|---|
| Chapters | 3 | Gatehouse, Service Tunnels, Summit Array |
| Relay objectives | 3 | One per chapter; frequency + repair interaction |
| Narrative clues | 6 | Two per chapter; optional but mechanically useful |
| Battery pickups | 3 | Unique, one per chapter |
| Threat variants | 1 antagonist with 6 states | Dormant, Investigate, Search, Pursue, Attack, Recede |
| Hide volumes | 4 | One gatehouse, two tunnels, one summit |
| Endings | 2 | Sever always available; Answer at 3+ clues |
| Meaningful verbs | 5 | Movement/look, flashlight, tune, repair, hide |
| Encounter beats | 3 | Signal reveal, tunnel pursuit, summit confrontation |

## Target experience
- Full run: 25–45 minutes.
- Required validation slice: at least 3 minutes with variation, escalation, and recovery.
- No combat, crafting, online account, or procedural story required for release slice.

## Escalation plan
1. Chapter 1: safe signal tutorial and first threat hint.
2. Chapter 2: active pursuit, hiding, and spatial distortion.
3. Chapter 3: high-risk final relay and ending decision.

## Scope reductions requiring a decision record
- Fewer than three chapters or fewer than two endings.
- Removing touch-first controls or offline save.
- Shipping visible greybox primitives in a final scene without an intentional-geometry decision.
